# Camera Calibration

## Hardware

- 5 Logitech Brio webcams
- 1920×1080
- 30 fps

## Calibration board

- ChArUco
- 7 × 10 squares
- 24 mm square length
- 18 mm marker length
- DICT_4X4_50

## Results

Intrinsic RMS

- cam0: 0.3705 px
- cam1: 0.5193 px
- cam2: 0.4372 px
- cam3: 0.4894 px
- cam4: 0.4133 px

Extrinsic calibration error

1.0325 px

Validation

- Mean reprojection error: 0.9995 px
- Median reprojection error: 0.7738 px
- p90 reprojection error: 2.0214 px

Scale validation

- Target: 24.000 mm
- Median: 23.992 mm
- Scale error: −0.033%
